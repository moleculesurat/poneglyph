## What it is
The Securities Market TechSprint 2026 is an innovation challenge organized by SEBI to harness
India's tech talent against real-world securities-market problems — empowering retail investors and
strengthening transparency, efficiency, compliance, and accessibility across India's capital markets.
Participants build a solution to **one** of four problem statements and showcase it at Global Fintech Fest.

## Evaluation process (3 rounds)
- **Round 01** — Preliminary shortlisting of applications (idea stage).
- **Round 02** — Shortlisting of working prototype + demo video.
- **Round 03** — Final evaluation (pitch at GFF).

## Evaluation criteria
1. **Market Impact** — potential to improve investor protection, market efficiency, compliance, or accessibility.
2. **Technology Stack** — effective use of AI, ML, Blockchain, NLP, DPI, advanced analytics.
3. **Feasibility** — real-world deployability and ease of implementation.
4. **Scalability** — scope of operation, users, transactions, velocity.
5. **Alignment with SEBI's Mandate** — investor protection, market development, supervision. *(seen weighted 20 in the event config)*
