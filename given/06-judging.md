## Five evaluation criteria
1. **Market Impact** — improves investor protection, market efficiency, compliance, or accessibility.
2. **Technology Stack** — effective use of AI, ML, Blockchain, NLP, DPI, advanced analytics.
3. **Feasibility** — real-world deployability and ease of implementation.
4. **Scalability** — scope of operation, users, transactions, velocity.
5. **Alignment with SEBI's Mandate** — investor protection, market development, supervision. *(weight 20 in event config)*

## Read-through for PS2 (us)
Two criteria are where PS2 wins or loses:
- **Alignment with SEBI's Mandate** — frame the solution as *supervision + investor protection* infra, not a
  generic LLM doc-chatbot. Tie outputs to real obligations from a named master circular.
- **Feasibility + auditability** — the PS explicitly demands "auditable compliance logic." Judges will look for
  an **audit trail / traceability** from regulatory clause → machine rule → evidence. That's the differentiator,
  not model size. Demonstrate on **one concrete regulatory scenario** (PS requirement).
