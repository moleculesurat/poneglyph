# 04 — Rules & Eligibility

## Official rules (verbatim)
1. Solutions must address one of the official problem statements announced by SEBI.
2. Teams may participate individually or with up to 5 members.
3. All submissions must be original and should not infringe on any intellectual property rights.
4. Participants are encouraged to leverage technologies such as AI, ML, Blockchain, NLP, DPI, and Advanced Analytics.
5. Teams must submit all required deliverables before the specified deadlines for each round.
6. Shortlisted teams must participate in the prototype demonstration and jury evaluation rounds.
7. Solutions should be practical, scalable, and applicable to real-world securities market challenges.
8. Participants must comply with all confidentiality, security, and data privacy requirements.
9. The decision of the jury and organizing committee will be final and binding.

## Eligibility
Open to:
- **Startups**
- **Students** — Undergraduate, Postgraduate, and Doctoral
- **Individuals** — including Technology Professionals, Market Researchers, and Innovators
- **Fintech, RegTech, WealthTech, and Capital Markets solution teams**

❌ **SEBI officials are NOT eligible to participate.**

## Team requirements
- Teams can consist of **1 to 5 members**.
