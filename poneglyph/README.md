# Poneglyph — Agentic Compliance Sandbox

**From regulatory text to operational action.** A frontend sandbox of Poneglyph, an agentic compliance engine for SEBI market intermediaries — built for **SEBI Securities Market TechSprint PS2 (Agentic Compliance)**.

### ▶ Live: **[poneglyph.walrussecuritas.com](https://poneglyph.walrussecuritas.com)**

<sub>Fallback: [poneglyph.techgenie2050.workers.dev](https://poneglyph.techgenie2050.workers.dev) · served as a static export from Cloudflare's edge — no server runtime, no cold starts.</sub>

SEBI circulars are the poneglyphs: the true text is public, and almost nobody can read all of it. The engine reads it, grounds every obligation to a paragraph, and keeps reading as the law changes.

> **Sandbox — explorable, not actionable.** Every surface expands, filters, replays and deep-links. No button writes anything: action controls fire the sandbox toast. The sim-clock is pinned to **2026-07-12**, so "SEBI amended Para 46 nine days ago" stays true forever.

One obligation graph, two windows — switch **Broker ↔ SEBI Inspector** from the profile menu at the bottom of the sidebar. The shell is a real-SaaS layout: grouped sidebar nav (Oversight / Compliance / Engine / Inspection), breadcrumbs + route-slug chip in the sticky header, tenant card, profile dropdown.

| Tab | Route | What it shows |
|-----|-------|---------------|

| **Entity Onboarding** | `/onboarding` | How Angel One entered the register: resolve the firm from public filings → infer segments → **compute** QSB and CSCRF → bind 9 of 10 Parts → derive the document asks → activate. Six replayable steps, 13 questions, the full provenance table |

The two vaults are deliberately distinct: **Evidence** is proof a control ran ("did you do it"); **Documents** is the source material the engine learned the firm from ("who are you").

## Architecture stance (what the real product does)

Two axes, maintained separately. The **corpus axis** is what the law says: a new circular becomes a new **job** through a fixed pipeline (`watch → applicability → extract → verify → human gate`), not a new free-roaming agent. The **entity axis** is who the firm is: onboarding resolves facts with provenance, computes designations, and binds scope. The register is the join of the two — change a fact and the register changes.

LLM workers are ephemeral. The **typed obligation ontology** (`lib/schema.ts`) is the permanent thing. A deterministic verifier checks every agent proposal before it enters the register, a human gate signs anything material, and the hash chain makes tampering unmissable rather than impossible.

What the engine cannot verify, it reports as unverified — never as compliant. Six QSB parameters are marked NOT COMPUTABLE rather than proxied; the FY2025-26 profit line is absent rather than estimated. An engine that fills gaps by inference is an engine that cannot be audited.

## Repo map

- `lib/schema.ts` — the open SEBI obligation ontology (the schema **is** the product)
- `lib/domains.ts` — the verified SEBI taxonomy: Parts I–X of the Master Circular (ref SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2024/53) with verbatim titles, the chapter → Part rollup, and the CSCRF / QSB overlay frameworks
- `lib/nav.ts` — route table, groups, inspector visibility
- `data/` — entity profile, onboarding session, document vault, corpus, register, evidence, runs, tasks, audit chain, MCP surface
- `app/` — one route per tab; `app/page.tsx` is the design exemplar
- `components/` + `app/globals.css` — the Poneglyph design system
- `DESIGN.md` — the build contract (brand rules, data contract, page specs)

## Worker secrets

Copy `dev.vars.example` to `.dev.vars` (gitignored) for `npx wrangler dev`; `GATE_TOKEN` gates the write routes and `KIMI_*` wires the extraction model.
On deploy, set them as Worker secrets: `npx wrangler secret put GATE_TOKEN` (and likewise `KIMI_API_KEY`, `KIMI_BASE_URL`, `KIMI_MODEL`).
